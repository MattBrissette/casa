No matter how compelling the photos—guests consistently remark that nothing prepares them for the grandeur of entering the open-concept great room and looking over the infinity pool to the ocean below. We invite you to experience the Pura Vida vibe of Casa la Onda in what we are certain will become a cherished memory that lasts a lifetime.

Great room and gardens

There is nothing quite like the feeling you are one with the natural environment, especially in Costa Rica where one experiences a symphony of birdsong, often accompanied by the distant roar of the howler monkeys. Casa la Onda’s open-air Balinese-inspired Great Room combines relaxation spaces for conversation along with kitchen and dining spaces that flow uninterrupted to the infinity pool and surrounding garden. While away the hours in the pool. Relax with a good book in the protective shade of the palapa. And as evening approaches, make the difficult choice of cooking in the well-appointed kitchen in the Great Room or grilling something marvelous in the newly renovated BBQ area. Either way, a spectacular sunset awaits.

Lower-floor bedrooms and baths

Each bedroom at Casa la Onda offers views over the grounds to the ocean. King-sized beds and air-conditioning make each room a pleasant retreat from the heat of the day and assures a good night’s rest. Each room also features a sitting area, a bathroom with double-sink vanities, and private outdoor covered shower and lavatory. Also, a powder room is conveniently located in the Great Room.

Casita la Onda

Casita la Onda is the kind of place where you can imagine Ernest Hemingway penning a novel. This lovely tiny house offers expansive views over a jungle valley. Couches by day become a king-sized bed or two single beds by night. A kitchenette features a mini-fridge and induction cooktop while the bathroom features impressive jungle views while you shower. Rent it separately, or as part of your stay at Casa la Onda.

The Experience

Access & Security

Casa la Onda is part of a small, private community with a security gate. Guests receive a code to this gate and also a code to the Casa compound gate. 4X4 vehicles or ATVs are required to access the property.

Vehicle Requirement: 4x4 or ATV for Hill Access
Concierge & Services

Our concierge service is available to assist you before and during your stay: car and ATV rentals, horseback rides at sunset, zip lining, massage or booking your own private chef. We are always at your service.
